# Customer Segmentation and Predictive Analytics

**Customer Segmentation and Predictive Analytics**  
Applied PCA and KMeans to segment customers by purchasing behavior. Built predictive models for churn risk and spending trends. Generated insights to support targeted marketing, customer retention, and revenue optimization.

---

## 🔍 Project Overview
- **Segmentation**: PCA and KMeans clustering to identify behavioral groups.  
- **Prediction**: Supervised models to estimate churn and spending patterns.  
- **Insights**: Actionable outputs for marketing, retention, and revenue growth.  

---

## 📊 Workflow
1. **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing** – removed duplicates, handled missing values, scaled features.  
2. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** – purchase behavior, correlations, visual insights.  
3. **Segmentation** – PCA for dimensionality reduction, KMeans clustering with validation.  
4. **Prediction** – churn probability and spending trend modeling.  
5. **Insights** – customer personas, retention strategies, targeted marketing recommendations.  

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Python**: pandas, numpy, matplotlib, seaborn  
- **Machine Learning**: scikit-learn (PCA, KMeans, silhouette, regression/classification)  
- **Visualization**: matplotlib, seaborn  
- **Environment**: Jupyter Notebook  

---

## 📈 Results
- Defined distinct customer segments.  
- Forecasted churn risk and future spending.  
- Provided business-ready insights for improved targeting and retention.
